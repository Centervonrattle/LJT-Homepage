Junteng Liu is the first author of this work, which studies the perception bottleneck of vision-language models (VLMs) for chart understanding. The code for this project is released in the `Vision4Chart` repository on GitHub. Bibtex and PDFs for this paper are also listed on my [Google Scholar profile](https://scholar.google.com/citations?hl=en&user=tbK9jl4AAAAJ&view_op=list_works&sortby=pubdate).
